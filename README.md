## Project Overview
The Home Security System is a work-in-progress systems engineering project focused on exploring a camera-free security solution that can distinguish authorized individuals from potential intruders using a combination of sensor-based measurements.
   The concept explores the use of biometric identification, physical measurements, and movement-related characteristics to help differentiate individuals and support access-control decisions.

## System Concept and Planned Sensors
The proposed system may incorporate the following sensors and components:

- **Tracking device:** Intended to support tracking or monitoring an individual's movement. Its specific role and implementation remain under investigation.
- **Biometric sensor:** Intended to explore fingerprint-based identification.
- **Ultrasonic sensor:** Intended to investigate distance measurement and its potential use in estimating a person's height.
- **Joystick sensor:** Under consideration; its intended function within the system has not yet been defined.
- **Capacitive weight sensor:** Under investigation for its potential role in measuring weight-related characteristics.

The overall concept is to explore whether a combination of biometric information, physical measurements, and movement characteristics could help distinguish individuals. The feasibility, accuracy, and integration of these methods will require further analysis and verification.

## Systems Engineering Approach
The project is being developed through a systems engineering approach, beginning with:

1. **Operational Analysis:** Exploring the operational environment, system purpose, and interactions between users and the proposed security system.
2. **System Analysis:** Investigating system needs, identifying potential requirements, and examining the functions needed to support the intended security objectives.

Future development will explore logical and physical architecture, hardware and software selection, component integration, and verification strategies as the project progresses.

## Technology Selection
Hardware platforms, sensors, programming languages, and communication technologies have not yet been finalized. Potential options will be evaluated as system requirements and architectural decisions become more clearly defined.

## Project Status
**Status: Work in Progress**

The project is currently focused on operational analysis, system analysis, and identifying potential sensors and technologies. Implementation decisions will follow as the system's requirements, architecture, and technical feasibility are investigated.
